02

The Lithium Limit: Thermodynamics

Lithium cell degradation is non-linear; it is exponential according to the Arrhenius Equation. A 10°C rise doubles the rate of side reactions, consuming active lithium and thickening the Solid-Electrolyte Interphase (SEI).

Critical Parasitic Loads

During heatwaves, HVAC systems consume up to 4% of a battery's nominal capacity. This represents losses of up to $21M per project over a 20-year lifespan in cooling energy alone.

85°C Stress: Immediate 7.5% capacity loss
120°C Thermal Runaway Risk: Irreversible loss
